  4. Power AI features — to process your inputs and generate responses through our AI assistant (see Section 4).
  5. Communicate with you — to send onboarding emails, product updates, service notices, and, where permitted, marketing communications you may opt out of at any time.
  6. Maintain security and prevent abuse — to detect, investigate, and prevent fraudulent, unauthorized, or illegal activity.
